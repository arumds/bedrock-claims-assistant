# Northwind Mutual - Health Plus Policy (policy prefix HLT)

## [Section 3.1] Emergency care
Emergency treatment is covered at in-network rates at any hospital, including out-of-network facilities, when the condition is a medical emergency.

## [Section 3.2] Pre-authorisation
Planned inpatient admissions require pre-authorisation. Pre-authorisation is not required for emergency admissions, but the insurer must be notified within 48 hours of admission or as soon as reasonably possible.

## [Section 3.3] Inpatient and surgical benefits
Medically necessary surgery and inpatient stays are reimbursed at 90% after the deductible, subject to an annual limit of EUR 50,000.

## [Section 3.4] Deductible
An annual deductible of EUR 250 applies per member.

## [Section 3.5] Out-of-network non-emergency care
Non-emergency care from out-of-network providers is reimbursed at 60%.

## [Section 3.6] Prescription drugs
Discharge medication is covered under the pharmacy benefit at 80%.
