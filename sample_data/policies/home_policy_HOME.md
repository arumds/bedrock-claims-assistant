# Northwind Mutual - Homeowners Policy (policy prefix HOME)

## [Section 2.1] Sudden and accidental water damage
We cover damage caused by a sudden and accidental discharge of water from a plumbing, heating or appliance system, including the cost of tearing out and replacing part of the building to reach the system.

## [Section 2.2] Gradual leakage and seepage exclusion
We do not cover damage caused by continuous or repeated leakage or seepage of water occurring over a period of 14 days or more, nor resulting rot, deterioration, mould or fungi, unless the leakage was hidden within walls or floors and could not reasonably have been known.

## [Section 2.3] Repair of the source
The cost of repairing the failed pipe, fitting or appliance itself is not covered; only the resulting damage is covered.

## [Section 2.4] Deductible
A deductible of EUR 500 applies to every property claim.

## [Section 2.5] Mould remediation limit
Where mould remediation is covered, payment is limited to EUR 2,500 per claim.

## [Section 2.6] Vacancy
If the home is unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days, water damage coverage is suspended unless the water supply was shut off.
