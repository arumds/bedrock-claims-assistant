# Northwind Mutual - Comprehensive Auto Policy (policy prefix AUTO)

## [Section 1.1] Collision coverage
We pay for direct and accidental physical damage to your covered vehicle caused by collision with another vehicle or object, including when your vehicle is parked or stationary.

## [Section 1.2] Third-party liability and subrogation
If another party is at fault, we pay your covered loss and then recover the amount from the at-fault party's insurer (subrogation). When subrogation succeeds, your deductible is refunded in full.

## [Section 1.3] Deductible
A collision deductible of EUR 300 applies to each claim. The deductible is waived when a police report confirms a third party was fully at fault.

## [Section 1.4] Rental car reimbursement
Rental car costs are covered at up to EUR 45 per day for a maximum of 10 days while the covered vehicle is being repaired.

## [Section 1.5] Claim requirements
Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident and include a repair estimate from a licensed repair shop and photographs of the damage. A police report is required for any collision involving another vehicle.

## [Section 1.6] Exclusions
We do not cover wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, damage while racing, or damage when the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
